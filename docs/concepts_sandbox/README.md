# Concepts Sandbox

Discoverability: index. Every member of this directory is listed below.
Member state: required. Every document declares date, updated date, owner and
status.

Excluded concept work: ideas, visions, sketches, mocks, rejected paths, and
imported material from the previous project. Nothing here is decided
direction, roadmap or current scope, and no task, specification or decision
record may cite anything here as authority.

The boundary is deliberately asymmetric. Material enters freely, with no
identity, no charter and no verification. It leaves only by being restated in
an owning document through an activated task or a decision record. Nothing
becomes true by sitting here, and nothing becomes true by being linked from
an authoritative document.

Every document states what it does not decide.
