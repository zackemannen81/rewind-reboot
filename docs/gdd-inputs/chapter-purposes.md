Derived brief. Compiled from `docs/design/echo.md`,

Every chapter is **three acts**.

## The Echo stage budget

Seven stages, one ladder for the whole game. A stage is spent once.

| Ch | Stages | Name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Recognition |
| 2 | 2, 3 | Contradiction, Foreknowledge |
| 3 | 4, 5 | Impossible history, Multiple actors |
| 4 | none | — |
| 5 | 6, 7 | Macro-loop recognition, Identity |

Chapter 4 spends no stage on purpose. It is where the player *acts* on the
model Chapter 3 forced, instead of receiving another revelation.

**Open, and the owner's call:** Stage 7, Identity, is the strongest scene in
the game and this puts it in Chapter 5. If Chapters 4 and 5 may not get built,
move it to the end of Chapter 3 and let 4 and 5 be extension. Decide before
Chapter 3 is written, not after.

## Chapter 1 — Apartment 4C

**Purpose.** Teach that a route which looks impossible is only unrehearsed.
Build the player's first, comfortable, wrong model: *I am in a time loop.*
Make Apartment 4C an image the player can recall exactly, because they will
return to it and it will have changed meaning.

**Echo budget:** stage 1 only. Every Echo shows something the player has
already done themselves. Act 1 uses no Echo at all.

**The chain, and do not replace it:** the radio broadcasts a code; the code
opens the 4C door; the fuse is one carried object contested between the lift
and the generator; committing `courtyard_gate_open` is the Anchor. Full rules
in the world-and-mechanics document.

**Three acts:**

1. **The room.** Wake in 4C. Learn the space, the radio's channels, the fuse
   lying on the desk, the locked door. The loop resets and nothing is lost but
   time. No Echo.
2. **The code.** Standing through the broadcast on channel 3 is the first thing
   the player does that is worth doing twice. The door opens. The first Echoes
   appear, doing things the player recognises as their own.
3. **Down and out.** Lift or stairs, decided by where the fuse is. The gate,
   the generator, the commit. The chapter ends when the Returner leaves for the
   Transit Hub.

**Ends with the player knowing:** the code, that channel 3 speaks it, roughly
when in the loop it speaks, that the fuse cannot be in two places, and that
Echoes exist and appear to be memories of their own loops.

**Planted, never explained:** the radio is already on channel 2. The fuse is
lying out as though left ready. The door carries marks from attempts nobody
made. Do not have anything acknowledge these.

## Chapter 2 — Conduit

**Purpose.** Break the comfortable model. The player leaves the room they
understood and finds a system that is larger, still deterministic, and full of
information that is deliberately false. The first Echo that cannot be theirs.

**Echo budget:** stages 2 and 3. Stage 2 is an Echo doing something the player
never did — best staged in Apartment 4C on a return, because the falsification
only bites in a space the player knows completely. Stage 3 is an Echo operating
something correctly that the player has not yet been taught.

**Design authority is `chapter-2-authored.md`** — five spaces, nine puzzles,
eight loops, an antagonist that responds by fixed table and never remembers,
and false information as a rule. Follow it. It is accepted and this brief does
not override it.

**Ends with the player suspecting:** the loops are not only theirs, and someone
else has been here — or they have, in a way they cannot account for.

---

## Chapter 3 — unwritten

**Echo budget:** stages 4 and 5. Stage 4 is an Echo in a place the player has
never been, which kills the local-loop model outright. Stage 5 is two Echoes in
one frame, which kills the idea that an Echo is a single person's memory.

No space, puzzle or fiction is decided. Do not invent them here; the chapter
needs its own design document first.

## Chapter 4 — unwritten

**Echo budget:** none. Deliberately. This is where the player acts on the macro
model and the chapter's tension comes from what they now do differently.

## Chapter 5 — unwritten

**Echo budget:** stages 6 and 7. Stage 7 is the reserved scene: an Echo already
in a room the player is entering for the first time, taking the route they are
about to take, avoiding a danger they have not seen, then stopping at a door,
turning to the camera, and being unmistakably the Returner. Then it does the
thing that causes the catastrophe that starts the next cycle. No dialogue.
